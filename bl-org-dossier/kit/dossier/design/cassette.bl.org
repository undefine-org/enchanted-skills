* In one breath
#+begin_lede
Deck takes its structure from the studio hardware of the late 1970s and
early 1980s — CARI's *Cassette Futurism*: a brushed aluminium chassis, a
charcoal glass faceplate, amber phosphor read-outs, a column of tiny lamps,
and controls that look like they take a thumb. The machine is not a website
about a machine; it is a machine. This file is the language's single source:
the cells below are =dossier.design.cassette=, holding tokens, faces, bevels,
component rules and motion, and the kit turns them into the CSS that dresses
every mockup marked =:design cassette=.
#+end_lede

* The idea
Cassette Futurism is not nostalgia for the future; it is the look of
equipment that expected to be used. That is the right register for a
workstation dashboard. Every element answers "what is this, and what happens
if I press it?" A lamp is lit or unlit. A channel has a number. A field is
recessed, a key is raised, a plate is a plate. The design language borrows
CARI's material vocabulary and refuses its costume: no fake wood veneer, no
decorative knobs that do nothing.

Three rules follow.

#+begin_principles
Material says state | Raised means actionable, recessed means read-only, lit means live. Never write what a bevel can say.
Labels are tape | Names, units and modes sit on small self-adhesive labels in mono capitals. Prose stays in Plex Sans.
One amber lamp | Accent is a signal, not a mood: one lit amber element per panel at rest; red and green only where they are facts.
#+end_principles

* Shape
Corners are close and radii step by surface. The deck's depth comes from two
bevels — a one-pixel highlight on top, a one-pixel shade below — plus a
recessed well for anything you read rather than press.

* Motion
A deck moves like hardware: lamps warm, keys give, needles settle, tape
travels. Nothing floats. Nothing loops except the two things that really are
continuous — the scan while data is live, and the reels while something is
being written.

** Contrast, measured
Every pair the language leans on is checked, light and dark: body and meta on
each ground, the amber as text, a filled key, each state tint, and the focus
ring on a field. A deck that is beautiful and unreadable is a prop.

#+begin_src beam-lisp :id contrast
(defn- lum [hex]
  (let [c (fn [i] (let [x (/ (erlang/binary_to_integer (subs hex i (+ i 2)) 16) 255.0)]
                    (if (<= x 0.03928) (/ x 12.92) (math/pow (/ (+ x 0.055) 1.055) 2.4))))
        r (c 1) g (c 3) b (c 5)]
    (+ (* 0.2126 r) (* 0.7152 g) (* 0.0722 b))))
(defn- ratio [a b]
  (let [x (lum a) y (lum b) hi (max x y) lo (min x y)]
    (/ (+ hi 0.05) (+ lo 0.05))))
(def pairs
  [[:ink :paper 4.5 "body on the bench"] [:secondary :card 4.5 "secondary on a card"]
   [:muted :card 4.5 "meta on a card"] [:ink :field 4.5 "values in a well"]
   [:amber :paper 4.5 "an amber stamp"] [:on-forest :forest 4.5 "a filled key"]
   [:success :success-bg 4.5 "done"] [:pending :pending-bg 4.5 "waiting"]
   [:danger :danger-bg 4.5 "failure"] [:neutral :neutral-bg 4.5 "neutral"]
   [:focus :field 3.0 "focus on a field"]])
(defn- failures [theme]
  (vec (for [[fg bg min what] pairs :when (< (ratio (get theme fg) (get theme bg)) min)]
         [what fg bg (erlang/round (* 100 (ratio (get theme fg) (get theme bg))))])))
(assert (empty? (failures light)) (pr-str (failures light)))
(assert (empty? (failures dark)) (pr-str (failures dark)))
{:light :ok :dark :ok :pairs (count pairs)}

(defn- keyframes
  "Each token plays at its real duration and curve; :loop tokens never rest."
  [m]
  (let [rest-ms (if (:loop m) 0 1200) total (+ (:ms m) rest-ms)
        p (fn [x] (str (/ (erlang/round (* 1000.0 (/ x total))) 10.0) "%"))
        e (str "animation-timing-function:" (:ease m))]
    (str "@keyframes fm-" (:name m) "{0%{" (:from m) ";" e "}"
         (when (:mid m) (str (p (/ (:ms m) 2)) "{" (:mid m) ";" e "}"))
         (p (:ms m)) "{" (:to m) "}"
         "100%{" (:to m) "}}"
         ".f-demo.fm-" (:name m) "{animation:fm-" (:name m) " " total "ms infinite}")))
